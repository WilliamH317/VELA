DECISIONS MADE

Brand direction: The team agreed that Meridian's repositioning should
emphasize "intelligence" and "decision-making" rather than "analytics"
and "data." Sarah noted that their sales team consistently hears prospects
say "we already have a reporting tool" — the rebrand must break this
association.

Timeline confirmed: 12-week timeline approved. Sarah flagged that their
annual customer conference is in Week 14, so all launch materials must
be finalized by Week 12 with zero slippage.

Survey scope: Marcus proposed surveying both current customers (200 sample)
and prospects who declined (50 sample) to understand the perception gap.
David confirmed the prospect list is available from their CRM.

Design constraints: Lisa raised that Meridian's current teal color has
strong brand equity with existing users. Recommendation: evolve the
palette rather than replace it entirely. Sarah agreed — "evolution,
not revolution."
