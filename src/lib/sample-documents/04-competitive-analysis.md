# Competitive Landscape Analysis: Meridian Analytics

## Purpose
Map the competitive positioning of Meridian's key competitors to identify differentiation opportunities for the rebrand. This analysis focuses on brand perception and messaging — not feature comparison.

## Competitor Profiles

### 1. RetailIQ
**Positioning:** "The analytics platform built for retail"
**Brand voice:** Technical, data-first, feature-heavy messaging
**Visual identity:** Dark blue and white, minimal design, dashboard-forward imagery
**Target audience:** Data analysts and BI teams at enterprise retailers
**Strengths:** Strong technical credibility, extensive integration ecosystem
**Weakness:** Messaging excludes C-suite decision makers. Brand feels like a tool, not a strategic partner.
**Opportunity for Meridian:** RetailIQ owns the "analyst" audience. Meridian can own the "decision-maker" audience by positioning above the data layer.

### 2. ShopMetrics Pro
**Positioning:** "Smarter decisions for modern retail"
**Brand voice:** Approachable, benefit-focused, light on technical depth
**Visual identity:** Bright green and orange, playful illustrations, lifestyle imagery
**Target audience:** SMB retailers and e-commerce operators
**Strengths:** Strong brand awareness in SMB segment, excellent onboarding experience
**Weakness:** Not taken seriously by enterprise buyers. Brand perception is "starter tool."
**Opportunity for Meridian:** ShopMetrics owns SMB. Meridian should explicitly signal enterprise-grade sophistication without ShopMetrics' casual tone.

### 3. Prism Intelligence
**Positioning:** "AI-powered retail intelligence"
**Brand voice:** Forward-looking, innovation-focused, heavy on AI messaging
**Visual identity:** Purple gradient, abstract geometric shapes, futuristic aesthetic
**Target audience:** Innovation-minded retail executives
**Strengths:** Strong buzz around AI capabilities, premium price positioning
**Weakness:** Over-promises on AI; customer reviews frequently mention "the AI features don't actually work as advertised." Brand trust is fragile.
**Opportunity for Meridian:** Prism's AI overpromise creates an opening for Meridian to position as "intelligence you can trust" — grounded insights, not speculative AI.

## Positioning Gap Analysis

The competitive landscape reveals a clear gap:
- **RetailIQ** owns "technical analytics for data teams"
- **ShopMetrics** owns "easy analytics for small retailers"
- **Prism** owns "AI-forward innovation"

**Nobody owns "trusted intelligence for retail decision-makers."**

This is Meridian's positioning opportunity. The rebrand should claim the intersection of:
- Enterprise credibility (unlike ShopMetrics)
- Decision-maker focus (unlike RetailIQ)
- Proven reliability (unlike Prism)

## Recommended Differentiation Strategy
Lead with "intelligence" not "analytics." Frame every message around the decision that gets made, not the data that enables it. Visual identity should feel premium and confident — not flashy, not sterile.

**Proposed positioning statement:**
"Meridian turns retail data into confident decisions — trusted by the teams who shape what retail looks like next."
