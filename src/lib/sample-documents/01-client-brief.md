# Client Brief: Meridian SaaS Rebrand

## Client
Meridian Analytics — B2B SaaS platform for mid-market retail analytics

## Project Overview
Meridian is repositioning from a "data dashboard tool" to an "intelligence platform for retail decision-makers." They need a complete brand refresh that communicates this shift to their target audience without alienating their existing 400+ enterprise accounts.

## Target Audience
- VP-level decision makers at mid-market retailers (200–2,000 employees)
- Chief Merchandising Officers and Heads of Retail Strategy
- Current users who identify Meridian as "the reporting tool" rather than a strategic partner

## Project Goals
1. Redefine brand positioning from utility tool to strategic intelligence platform
2. Launch updated visual identity including logo, color palette, and typography
3. Develop messaging framework that resonates with C-suite buyers
4. Create launch campaign targeting 50 net-new enterprise prospects

## Key Deliverables
- Brand strategy document with positioning statement and messaging hierarchy
- Visual identity system (logo suite, color palette, typography, icon library)
- Brand guidelines PDF (40–60 pages)
- Launch landing page with demo request flow
- Sales enablement deck (12–15 slides)
- Social media launch kit (LinkedIn, Twitter templates)

## Timeline
- Discovery & Research: Weeks 1–2
- Brand Strategy: Weeks 3–4
- Visual Identity Development: Weeks 5–7
- Collateral Production: Weeks 8–10
- Launch Preparation: Weeks 11–12

## Budget Range
$85,000 – $110,000 (inclusive of strategy, design, and production)

## Success Metrics
- Brand perception shift measured via post-launch survey (target: 40% of respondents associate Meridian with "intelligence" over "reporting")
- Demo request conversion rate increase of 15% within 90 days of launch
- Sales cycle reduction from 45 days to 35 days average
