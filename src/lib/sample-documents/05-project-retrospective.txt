Project Retrospective: Cascade Fitness Rebrand (Q4 2025)

Project Lead: Lisa Torres
Date: January 15, 2026
Engagement Duration: 10 weeks (originally scoped for 8)

OVERVIEW
Cascade Fitness hired us to rebrand from a regional gym chain identity
to a national wellness platform positioning. Final deliverables included
brand strategy, visual identity, launch landing page, and sales deck.

WHAT WENT WELL

Client relationship: Weekly check-ins with their CMO kept alignment
tight. The client felt heard throughout the process, which their feedback
survey confirmed — 9.2/10 on "felt like a true partner."

Brand strategy phase: The discovery research was thorough. We interviewed
12 stakeholders (5 internal, 7 customers) and the insights directly
shaped the positioning statement. The client approved the strategy
document on the first review round — zero revisions needed.

Cross-team collaboration: The strategist-designer handoff worked smoothly
this time. Marcus' strategy brief included specific visual direction
notes that Lisa's team could act on immediately. This saved an estimated
week of back-and-forth.

Deliverable quality: Client rated final deliverables 9.5/10. The brand
guidelines document was called "the best we've ever received" in their
feedback survey.

WHAT TO IMPROVE

Scope creep on collateral: The original SOW included a 12-slide sales
deck. By Phase 4, the client had expanded this to a 28-slide deck plus
a leave-behind PDF. We absorbed the extra work without a change order,
which added 2 weeks to the timeline and reduced our effective margin
by approximately 12%.

Recommendation: Enforce change order process for any deliverable that
exceeds the original scope by more than 25%. Build this threshold into
the SOW template.

Revision tracking: We lost track of which feedback was from which
stakeholder during the visual identity phase. Two conflicting rounds
of revisions were submitted simultaneously, causing confusion.

Recommendation: Designate a single client-side feedback consolidator
in the SOW. All revision requests flow through one person.

Timeline estimation: We underestimated the collateral production phase
by 2 weeks. The landing page development took longer than expected
because the client's CMS required custom integration work.

Recommendation: Add a technical discovery step for any deliverable
that involves client-side systems. Estimate development time after
the technical review, not before.

PROCESS NOTES

Total billable hours: 412 (budget was 380)
Effective hourly rate: $218 (target was $250)
Client satisfaction score: 9.3/10
Net Promoter Score: 9 (promoter)
Likelihood of repeat engagement: High — already discussing Phase 2

KEY TAKEAWAY
The strategy and creative work was excellent. The margin erosion came
entirely from scope management in the production phase. Fix the change
order process and this becomes a highly profitable engagement model.
