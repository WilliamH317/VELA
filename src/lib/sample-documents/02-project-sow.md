# Statement of Work: Meridian Analytics Rebrand

## Engagement Overview
This Statement of Work defines the scope, deliverables, milestones, and payment schedule for the Meridian Analytics brand repositioning project.

## Scope of Work

### Phase 1: Discovery & Research (Weeks 1–2)
- Stakeholder interviews (5 internal, 3 customer)
- Competitive landscape audit (8 competitors)
- Brand perception survey deployment and analysis
- Deliverable: Discovery Report with findings and strategic recommendations

### Phase 2: Brand Strategy (Weeks 3–4)
- Positioning workshop with Meridian leadership team
- Messaging framework development (primary, secondary, tertiary messages)
- Audience persona refinement
- Deliverable: Brand Strategy Document with positioning statement and messaging hierarchy
- Milestone review: Client approval required before Phase 3

### Phase 3: Visual Identity (Weeks 5–7)
- 3 logo concept directions presented
- Color palette and typography selection
- Icon library design (24 custom icons)
- Deliverable: Visual Identity System and Brand Guidelines PDF
- Milestone review: Client approval on selected direction before production

### Phase 4: Collateral Production (Weeks 8–10)
- Launch landing page (design + development)
- Sales enablement deck
- Social media template kit
- Deliverable: All production files and assets

### Phase 5: Launch Support (Weeks 11–12)
- Launch day coordination
- Social media scheduling
- Performance tracking setup
- Deliverable: Launch Report with initial metrics

## Out of Scope
- Paid advertising creative or media buying
- Website redesign beyond the launch landing page
- Video production
- Ongoing social media management post-launch

## Payment Schedule
| Milestone | Amount | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Project kickoff | $25,000 | Week 1 |
| Strategy approval | $25,000 | Week 4 |
| Identity approval | $25,000 | Week 7 |
| Final delivery | $20,000 | Week 12 |

## Revision Policy
Each deliverable includes two rounds of revisions. Additional revision rounds billed at $175/hour.

## Team Allocation
- Creative Director: 20% allocation
- Senior Designer: 60% allocation
- Brand Strategist: 40% allocation (Phases 1–2), 10% (Phases 3–5)
- Project Manager: 15% allocation throughout
