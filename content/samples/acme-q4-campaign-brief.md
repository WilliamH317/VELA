## Campaign Overview

Acme Industries is launching a Q4 digital campaign to drive awareness and lead generation for their new enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform, AcmeOS 3.0. This campaign targets mid-market operations leaders who are evaluating ERP solutions for FY2027 budget planning.

## Objectives

1. **Awareness:** Generate 2,500+ qualified impressions among mid-market operations leaders (companies with 200–1,000 employees)
2. **Lead Generation:** Capture 180 marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) through gated content and demo requests
3. **Pipeline Contribution:** Source $450K in new pipeline by end of Q4
4. **Brand Positioning:** Establish Acme Industries as the "ERP built for operations teams, not IT departments"

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** VP of Operations / COO at mid-market companies (200–1,000 employees)
- **Secondary:** Operations Managers and Process Improvement leads evaluating tools for their leadership
- **Industries:** Manufacturing, logistics, professional services, healthcare administration
- **Geography:** United States, with emphasis on Midwest and Southeast markets

## Key Messages

- "AcmeOS 3.0 — Built for the people who actually run the business"
- Operations teams deserve tools designed for their workflows, not adapted from IT systems
- 40% faster implementation than legacy ERP platforms
- ROI visible within 90 days of deployment
