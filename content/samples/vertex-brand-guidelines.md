## Brand Overview

Vertex Corp provides mid-market financial operations software that helps growing companies manage their finances without the complexity of enterprise platforms. Our brand should reflect confidence, clarity, and approachability — we are the trusted guide that makes financial operations feel simple, not the intimidating vendor that makes them feel overwhelming.

## Brand Values

1. **Clarity** — We cut through complexity. Every interaction, from product to marketing, should make things clearer, not more confusing.
2. **Momentum** — We help businesses move forward. Our brand should feel energetic and progressive, never static or bureaucratic.
3. **Trust** — Financial data is sensitive. Our brand must communicate reliability, security, and professionalism at every touchpoint.
4. **Humanity** — Behind every financial operation is a person. We design for people, not personas.

## Voice Attributes

| Attribute | What It Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Confident** | We know our space. No hedging or uncertainty. | "Vertex closes your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks." |
| **Clear** | Plain language, short sentences, no jargon. | "See where your money goes" not "Gain visibility into expenditure allocation" |
| **Warm** | Friendly and approachable, not corporate. | "We're glad you're here" not "Welcome to the Vertex platform" |
| **Direct** | Get to the point. Respect the reader's time. | Lead with the benefit, then explain how. |

### Tone Adjustments by Context

- **Marketing website:** Confident and aspirational. Paint the picture of what's possible.
- **Product UI:** Clear and helpful. Guide without patronizing. Error messages should be human.
- **Sales materials:** Confident and specific. Use numbers, case studies, proof points.
- **Support content:** Warm and patient. Assume the reader is frustrated. Lead with empathy.
- **Legal/compliance:** Professional and precise. Clarity over warmth.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertex Blue | #2B5EA7 | 43, 94, 167 | Primary brand color, CTAs, headers |
| Vertex Navy | #1A2E4A | 26, 46, 74 | Text, dark backgrounds |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | Backgrounds, reverse text |

### Secondary Colors

| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Success Green | #34A853 | 52, 168, 83 | Positive states, confirmations |
| Alert Amber | #F5A623 | 245, 166, 35 | Warnings, attention states |
| Error Red | #D93025 | 217, 48, 37 | Errors, critical alerts |
| Cool Gray | #6B7280 | 107, 114, 128 | Secondary text, borders, dividers |

### Usage Rules

- Vertex Blue should appear on every page/screen as the dominant brand color
- Never use Vertex Blue for error states (use Error Red)
- Maintain minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for text on backgrounds (WCAG AA)
- Dark mode: swap Navy backgrounds to #0F1A2E, keep Vertex Blue as-is

## Typography

| Use | Font | Weight | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headings (h1–h3) | Inter | Bold (700) | 32px / 24px / 20px |
| Body text | Inter | Regular (400) | 16px |
| Captions & labels | Inter | Medium (500) | 14px |
| Code / data | JetBrains Mono | Regular (400) | 14px |

- Line height: 1.5 for body text, 1.2 for headings
- Maximum line length: 75 characters for readability
- Never use all-caps for body text (headings only, sparingly)

## Logo Usage

### Clear Space

- Minimum clear space around the logo: 1x the height of the "V" mark
- Never place the logo on busy backgrounds without a container

### Minimum Sizes

- Digital: 120px wide minimum
- Print: 1 inch wide minimum

### Don'ts

- Do not stretch, rotate, or skew the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside the approved palette
- Do not add drop shadows, gradients, or effects to the logo
- Do not place the logo on low-contrast backgrounds
- Do not recreate the logo in a different font

## Photography & Imagery

- Feature real people in real work environments (not stock photo offices)
- Emphasize diversity in age, ethnicity, and work context
- Lighting should feel natural and warm — avoid harsh corporate lighting
- Show people using technology, not just staring at screens
- Avoid: handshakes, pointed fingers at screens, people in suits standing around whiteboards
