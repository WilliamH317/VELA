## Situation Analysis

Beacon Industries manufactures commercial LED lighting for offices, retail spaces, and warehouses. Despite strong product-market fit and a growing customer base, Beacon's social media presence is minimal — sporadic posts on LinkedIn (last post: November 2025), an inactive Twitter/X account, and no Instagram or YouTube presence.

**Current State:**
- LinkedIn: 842 followers, 2–3 posts/month (irregular), avg. 12 engagements per post
- Twitter/X: 214 followers, last post October 2025
- Instagram: No account
- YouTube: 3 product videos uploaded in 2024, no consistent publishing

**Competitor Benchmark:**
- Luminary LED (direct competitor): 4,200 LinkedIn followers, posts 4x/week, avg. 85 engagements
- BrightPath Lighting: Active YouTube channel with installation guides (12K subscribers)
- ClearBeam: Strong Instagram presence showcasing installations (8,500 followers)

## Proposed Strategy

### Goals (6-Month Horizon)

1. Grow LinkedIn followers from 842 to 2,500+
2. Achieve consistent 3%+ engagement rate across all platforms
3. Generate 25+ inbound inquiries per month from social channels
4. Position Beacon as a thought leader in commercial LED innovation

### Platform Recommendations

| Platform | Priority | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Primary | B2B decision makers, facility managers, architects |
| **Instagram** | Secondary | Visual showcase of installations, project case studies |
| **YouTube** | Tertiary | Installation guides, product demos, customer testimonials |
| Twitter/X | Deprioritize | Low ROI for B2B manufacturing, maintain minimal presence |

### Content Pillars

1. **Product Innovation** (30%) — New product launches, feature highlights, technical specs made accessible
2. **Installation Showcases** (25%) — Before/after photos, project case studies, customer spotlights
3. **Industry Insights** (25%) — Energy efficiency trends, sustainability regulations, workplace wellness data
4. **Company Culture** (20%) — Team spotlights, manufacturing behind-the-scenes, community involvement
