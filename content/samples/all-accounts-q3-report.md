## Areas for Improvement

1. **Harrison Group:** NPS dropped to 48. Root cause: misalignment on retainer scope — they expected more strategic input, we delivered execution. **Action:** James Whitfield scheduling scope realignment meeting for October 15.
2. **Hours overrun on Henderson & Meridian:** Both accounts saw 12-13% overruns due to client-requested scope additions that weren't captured in change orders. **Action:** Mandatory change order process for any request exceeding 4 hours starting Q4.
3. **Creative team capacity:** Andre Kim and Kenji Tanaka are at 115% utilization. **Action:** Evaluating freelance design support for Q4 peak season.
