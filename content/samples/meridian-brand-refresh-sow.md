## Project Overview

Meridian Group is undergoing a brand refresh to modernize their visual identity and messaging as they expand from regional accounting services into national advisory and consulting. This SOW covers the full brand refresh engagement from discovery through final asset delivery.

## Scope of Work

### Phase 1: Discovery & Audit (Weeks 1–2)

- Stakeholder interviews (6 interviews: CEO, CFO, VP Sales, VP Marketing, 2 senior partners)
- Competitive brand audit (5 direct competitors, 3 aspirational brands)
- Client perception survey (distributed to top 25 clients, minimum 15 responses)
- Current asset inventory and brand consistency assessment
- Discovery summary presentation to leadership team

### Phase 2: Strategy & Positioning (Weeks 3–4)

- Brand positioning framework (purpose, promise, personality, pillars)
- Messaging architecture (elevator pitch, boilerplate, tagline options x3)
- Audience persona refinement (3 personas: enterprise CFO, mid-market controller, startup founder)
- Tone of voice guidelines with before/after copy examples

### Phase 3: Visual Identity (Weeks 5–8)

- Logo redesign (3 concepts → 1 selected → 2 rounds of refinement)
- Color palette (primary, secondary, accent, digital-safe variants)
- Typography system (headings, body, accent — web and print)
- Photography and illustration direction
- Icon set (24 custom icons for web and presentations)

### Phase 4: Brand Guidelines & Rollout (Weeks 9–10)

- Brand guidelines document (40–60 pages, PDF + interactive)
- PowerPoint and Google Slides master templates
- Email signature templates
- Social media profile assets (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram)
- Brand launch internal memo and talking points
