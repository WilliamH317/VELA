## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for onboarding new clients from signed contract through first deliverable. A consistent onboarding experience sets the tone for the entire engagement and reduces the "where do I find...?" questions that slow down the first 30 days.

## Process Overview

**Total Duration:** 10 business days from signed SOW to first working session
**Responsible:** Account Director (primary), Project Manager (secondary)

## Step-by-Step Checklist

### Day 1: Contract & Setup

- [ ] Signed SOW received and filed in `Contracts/[Client Name]/` shared drive folder
- [ ] Client added to billing system (QuickBooks) with payment terms from SOW
- [ ] Client Slack channel created: `#client-[name]` (internal) and `#ext-[name]` (shared with client)
- [ ] Client folder structure created in Google Drive (use template: `Templates/New Client Folder`)
- [ ] Project created in Asana with SOW milestones as tasks
- [ ] Account Director sends welcome email (use template: `Templates/Welcome Email`)

### Day 2–3: Internal Briefing

- [ ] Account Director schedules 30-min internal briefing with assigned team
- [ ] Briefing covers: client background, SOW scope, key contacts, communication preferences, known sensitivities
- [ ] Team members confirm availability against project timeline
- [ ] Any resource conflicts escalated to Director of Operations (Marcus Rivera)

### Day 4–5: Client Kickoff Prep

- [ ] Kickoff deck prepared (use template: `Templates/Kickoff Deck`)
- [ ] Deck includes: team introductions, communication plan, project timeline, first 30-day milestones, Q&A section
- [ ] Access requests sent to client: shared drives, brand assets, CMS, analytics, ad accounts
- [ ] Calendar invites sent for kickoff meeting and recurring syncs

### Day 6–7: Kickoff Meeting

- [ ] Kickoff meeting conducted (60 min, video call preferred)
- [ ] Attendees: Account Director, Project Manager, lead strategist/creative, client primary contact + stakeholders
- [ ] Meeting recorded and link shared in client Slack channel
- [ ] Action items documented and sent within 2 hours of meeting

### Day 8–10: First Sprint Launch

- [ ] Client assets collected and organized (logos, brand guidelines, credentials, past work)
- [ ] First deliverable sprint begins per project plan
- [ ] Project Manager sends Day 10 check-in email: "Here's where we are, here's what we need from you"
- [ ] Account Director schedules 15-min "temperature check" call with client primary contact

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Skipping the internal briefing** — Teams that jump straight to kickoff without context waste the first 2 weeks asking questions the Account Director already knows the answer to.
2. **Not collecting assets early** — Waiting until design starts to request logos, brand guides, and credentials adds 3–5 days of delays. Request on Day 4, follow up on Day 6.
3. **Too many people at kickoff** — Cap kickoff at 6–8 people. Large meetings produce vague action items. If the client wants to include 10+ people, suggest a separate "meet the team" session.
4. **No recurring sync cadence** — If weekly syncs aren't on the calendar by Day 7, communication gaps appear by Week 3. Always schedule recurring meetings during kickoff.
5. **Unclear decision authority** — Confirm who has final sign-off authority at kickoff. "I need to check with my team" on every deliverable signals we missed this step.

## Escalation

If onboarding cannot be completed within 10 business days (e.g., client unresponsive, missing assets, resource conflicts), the Account Director escalates to Marcus Rivera with a brief written summary of the blocker and proposed resolution.
