## Retainer Overview

Harrison Group engages our agency on a monthly retainer for ongoing marketing strategy, content creation, and campaign management. This document defines the scope, deliverables, and terms of the retainer arrangement.

## Monthly Deliverables

### Strategy & Planning (10 hours/month)

- Monthly strategy call with Harrison leadership team (1 hour)
- Quarterly strategic review and planning session (3 hours, billed in quarter month)
- Campaign performance analysis and optimization recommendations
- Competitive monitoring and market intelligence briefs (as needed)

### Content Creation (20 hours/month)

- Blog posts: 2 long-form articles (800–1,200 words each)
- Email marketing: 2 newsletter editions (design + copy)
- Social media: 8 LinkedIn posts (copy + graphics)
- One additional content asset per month (case study, infographic, or whitepaper outline — rotates quarterly)

### Campaign Management (10 hours/month)

- LinkedIn ad campaign management (ongoing optimization)
- Google Ads management (search campaigns, monthly keyword refresh)
- Monthly performance report with recommendations
- Ad creative refresh (1 set of new creatives per quarter)

### Total Monthly Hours: 40

## Hours & Overage Policy

- **Included hours:** 40 hours/month
- **Unused hours:** Do not roll over to the following month
- **Overage rate:** $175/hour, billed in 30-minute increments
- **Overage approval:** Any task expected to push the month over 40 hours requires written approval from Harrison Group's VP Marketing (David Park) before work begins
- **Emergency exception:** Up to 5 overage hours may be executed without prior approval for time-sensitive opportunities (e.g., reactive social media, urgent press inquiries). Harrison Group is notified within 24 hours.

## Reporting Format

Monthly reports are delivered by the 5th business day of the following month and include:

1. **Executive summary** — 3–5 bullet points on key wins and focus areas
2. **Channel performance** — LinkedIn, Google Ads, email, blog metrics with month-over-month trends
3. **Content scorecard** — Each deliverable with engagement metrics and recommendations
4. **Hours log** — Time spent by category (strategy, content, campaign) vs. allocated
5. **Next month preview** — Planned content calendar and campaign adjustments

Reports are delivered as a PDF with an accompanying 15-minute walkthrough video (Loom).

## Renewal Terms

- **Auto-renewal:** Agreement auto-renews for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides 60 days written notice before term end
- **Rate adjustment:** Agency may propose rate adjustments at renewal with 60 days notice. Current monthly retainer: $7,000/month
- **Early termination:** Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice. Client responsible for fees through the notice period.
- **Scope changes:** Material scope changes during the term require a written amendment signed by both parties
